SQL Business Analysis

Business Problem

Management needed insights on revenue trends and customer purchasing behavior.

Approach

Used advanced SQL queries including joins, aggregations, and window functions.

Sample Query


SELECT customer_id,
       SUM(amount) AS total_spent
FROM transactions
GROUP BY customer_id
ORDER BY total_spent DESC;

Impact

Identified top 20% revenue-generating customers.
